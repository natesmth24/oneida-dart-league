LEAGUE INFO

Rules

Regular Season

  • The regular season consists of 15 weeks of play.
  • Matches are played on Wednesday nights.
  • Each team must have four players to play a match.
  • Teams receive $2 for each individual game won during the regular season.
  • Weekly player statistics and team standings will be posted on this website.

Rosters and Substitutes

  • A maximum of two substitutes are permitted per team per regular-season match.
  • A player rostered on one team cannot substitute for another team.
  • Unrostered substitutes may play for multiple teams during the regular season.
  • Roster changes must be reported to the league organizer.

Make-Up Matches

  • Postponed matches must be completed within two weeks of the original scheduled date.
  • All make-up matches must be completed before the final regular-season match.
  • Both teams must notify the league organizer of any rescheduled match.
  • If captains cannot agree on a make-up date, contact the league organizer.

Standings and Tiebreakers

  • Teams are ranked according to the league standings.
  • If two teams are tied, their head-to-head record determines the higher seed.
  • Three-way ties will be reviewed by the league organizer.

Playoffs

  • All six teams qualify for the playoffs.
  • The top two seeds receive first-round byes.
  • First-round matchups are seed #3 vs. #4 and seed #5 vs. #6.
  • Seed #1 plays the lowest remaining seed in the semifinals.
  • The higher-seeded team hosts each playoff match.
  • Playoff matches are played until one team wins 10 games.
  • The championship match is played at the end-of-season banquet.

Playoff Substitute Eligibility

  • Substitutes must have played at least five regular-season matches to be playoff eligible.
  • A maximum of one substitute is permitted per team during a playoff match.

Player Order

  • The higher-seeded team determines player order during playoff matches.
